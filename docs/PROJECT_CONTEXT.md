## What this project is
Master's thesis (TU/e DS&AI): a **diagnostic evaluation framework for time-series–text alignment**. Three controlled probes (compositional component-swap / order-invariance shuffle / summary-statistics sufficiency) applied to four models (CLaSP reimplementation, TRACE, ChatTS, text-embedding-3-large) to attribute retrieval performance to shortcuts vs. non-shortcut alignment behavior.

**Core principle:** never read absolute metric values. Every probe result is *relative degradation vs. the same model's unperturbed baseline*, with paired statistics (bootstrap CIs, Wilcoxon/McNemar, Holm–Bonferroni across Probe-1's five components, TOST equivalence tests when claiming absence of degradation).

## Binding decisions (do not reopen without explicit discussion)
- **Thesis prose says "diagnostic", never "probe"** (decided 2026-08-17, supervisor-flagged: "probe" collides with the established probing-classifier meaning in ML/NLP, which names a different method — training a classifier on frozen representations; this project perturbs inputs and measures behaviour). Scope: prose only. Repository artifact names (`probe1/2/3`, scripts, JSON keys, prediction IDs P2-1/P3-4/PC1-1 …) are **unchanged** — do not rename files or keys. The thesis carries a one-line mapping note ("the diagnostics are named probe1–3 in the repository"). Chapter drafts under `docs/thesis_drafts/` already follow this.
- **Probe-facing metric is strict pair-level retrieval** (Recall@k, MRR against the ground-truth pairing). The paper's soft judge-based mAP@10 is used **only** for reproduction comparison — it saturates (one published configuration accepts 99.7% of candidate pairs; a random-init model scores 0.999 under it) and therefore cannot register degradation.
- **Primary metrics are MRR and Recall@10.** Seed noise floor: R@10 3.5%, MRR 5.6%, R@1 11.6% overall and **32% on SUSHI alone**. Recall@1 is reported but no conclusion rests on it.
- **Every probe runs against all three checkpoints** (seeds 42/43/44). Significance comes from *paired* tests within each seed; cross-seed agreement is reported as replication. The seed noise floor is a conservative outer bound, not the significance test.
- **Bootstrap resamples SIGNALS, not items.** Probe items share signals (~20 items per signal); resampling items would shrink intervals several-fold and manufacture significance.
- **Equivalence margin ±0.05** for claiming absence of degradation, justified by the Phase-1a seed noise floor. It was fixed *after* the Probe-1 point estimates were seen; that must be stated in the thesis. Do not revise it retrospectively.
- **Probe 1 items are binary forced choice** with a matched random-distractor control per item. Binary because component pools differ in size, so a k-way pool would give each component a different chance level.
- **Probe 2 metric is strict retrieval rank shift** (decided 2026-08-09): perturbed vs unperturbed rank of the ground-truth caption, MRR and R@10 primary, paired per query, against each model's frozen unperturbed baseline. Forced choice was considered and rejected — Probe 1's unequal-pool justification does not transfer (Probe 2 swaps nothing caption-side), and a random-distractor forced choice starts at the 0.95–0.99 ceiling, compressing the difference-in-differences. Per-group unperturbed baselines are reported alongside every DiD. The floor model's Probe-2 verdict is **pre-declared VOID** (no capability to degrade); it runs as the pipeline's negative control.
- **Probe-2 caption grouping is truth-conditional** (decided 2026-08-09): a caption is order-dependent if shuffling the signal makes ANY of its claims false; order-invariant means zero order-sensitive claims anywhere; ambiguous-excluded is reserved for genuinely unclassifiable language and is counted. Purity subgroups reported secondarily if numbers allow. Permutation fixed points (SUSHI `clean; constant`) form a separate DEGENERATE bucket — identity controls, never inside a DiD group.
- **TRACE's Probe-2 conclusion-carrier is the perturbation profile, not the caption-group DiD** (accepted 2026-08-10): the census certified that TRACE descriptions are saturated with order language (2,005/2,006 dependent, 0 invariant — the DiD is unposable, recorded as a finding). The diagnostic for TRACE is therefore the degradation profile across perturbation types — shuffle-family (destroys order, preserves values) vs masking (destroys values, largely preserves order) — using only already-committed machinery. The caption-group DiD remains the primary diagnostic where the caption distribution permits it (TRUCE; SUSHI descriptively).
- **Probe-2 perturbation mechanics** (accepted 2026-08-10): SUSHI point-level (parent's definition; `sf_patchs` shelved as a named optional extra); TRUCE 12-point definitions (sf-all permutes 12, sf-half permutes first 6, ex-half swaps the 6-halves); TRACE channels shuffled JOINTLY (same permutation across channels, parent precedent `[:, perm, :]`; independent shuffling would confound order-destruction with cross-channel-alignment destruction) with per-signal seeded draws as the documented adaptation of the parent's per-batch draw; masking ratio **0.2** pre-registered by us (parent code default; the paper's reported ratio is unpinned), fill 0, random non-contiguous positions — for TRACE this is ADDITIONAL to the standing 0.3 protocol mask and is reported as 0.3+0.2, never as bare 0.2. All perturbations test-time only, per the parent.
- **Probe-2 runner gates G1–G9** (accepted 2026-08-13, Q5; full text in handoff §4.6 item 5): fatal policy is fixed — G3 (grouping counts vs certified), G4 (known-dependent stratum must not improve under sf-all), G6 (frozen-baseline digit-exact reproduction before any perturbed number), G7 (permutation validity), G9 (lossless per-query pairing) HARD-STOP; G1 no-ops (ex-half fixed points) and G2 natural-zero rates are reported, never fatal — they are properties of the data, not defects of the run.
- **TRUCE Probe-2 grouping is census-certified (2026-08-13)** — do not regroup or re-litigate: dependent 7,089 rows / 4,862 texts / 715 test; invariant 245 / 185 / 18; ambiguous 46 / 40 / 5; degenerate series 0. Canonical artifact `results/analysis/probe2_truce_groups_certified.json` (rules v2 + Ranyi's full census; eight human moves recorded inside it with provenance). **P2-8 scoring was pinned pre-run** (parseable = dependent + invariant; row-level population primary) **and the prediction MISSED: 0.0334 vs 0.15** — recorded, with v1/v2 provisionals retained; the miss is reported as the three-substrate saturation finding, never quietly dropped. The DiD's test-split invariant group is 18 rows: the TOST leg is always quoted with its n.
- **Probe-2 CLaSP mechanics and metric (accepted 2026-08-13, binding):** M1 masking fills 0 on the z-normed input; M2 int(0.2·L) masked points (SUSHI 409/2048, TRUCE 2/12 reported as 16.7% effective); M3 per-signal seeds sha256(sample_id|pert|ckpt_seed). D1: G6a = SUSHI metrics digit-exact vs frozen (hard stop — not tie-explainable); G6b = TRUCE-containing metrics within 6e-3 (tie bound). D2: the Probe-2 rank metric is DETERMINISTIC AVERAGE RANK (exact ties get their mean position); legacy argsort rank exists only for the G6 frozen-table comparison. TOST margin for P2-5 was pinned PRE-ANALYSIS at ±0.05 (mirrors P2-1) — never adjust post hoc. **D2-F (accepted 2026-08-14, scoped to the floor arm):** exact ties are detected at CONSTRUCTION level — pool signals with identical serialisation strings (hence identical cached vectors) have their similarity COLUMNS equalised before ranking, because BLAS column paths split bitwise-identical vectors at ~1 ulp (22 of 24 forced ties found by float equality; `scripts/diagnose_floor_ties.py`). Float-level counts are always printed alongside; copying vectors instead of columns does NOT fix it. P2-4 scoring was pinned pre-run (inference cells = the two dependent groups; TOST ±0.05 abs; thin cells reported-with-n, never load-bearing; DiD reported with decomposition + leave-out sensitivity, never a verdict input) and **P2-4 is CONFIRMED — not re-litigated**. Scored verdicts (P2-1/2/3 CONFIRMED, P2-5 MISSED) are recorded and not re-litigated; the certified TRUCE grouping was NOT reopened by the P2-5 drill-down.
- **A shortcut claim requires high random-condition accuracy.** Where both conditions sit near chance the verdict is **VOID**, not "degraded" — there is no capability for a perturbation to degrade. Encoded in `analyze_probe1_stats.py`; the threshold (interval lower bound < 0.60) is a convention, so borderline cases must be argued rather than read off the label.
- **Every model's results get the length-vs-margin diagnostic** (`audit_item_balance.py --results ...`). It is what turned the caption-length confound from bounded into closed: CLaSP +0.023 in the swap condition against the floor baseline's +0.174 over identical items.
- **Probe 1 substrate:** SUSHI primary (rule-based parse of class labels); TRUCE secondary (report parse-coverage rate, state selection bias as a limitation).
- **ChatTS arm (accepted 2026-08-15/16; handoff §4.9 holds the full record):** checkpoint pinned to the PAPER-ERA revision `1e661101dcfff86dc66f3397336b85f2f1cc5e89` of `bytedance-research/ChatTS-14B` (HF main was replaced in place by 0801 — do not use main). Both answer orders per item; one MCQ template across probes (sha `4029f94e2f6d`; five-number `4a4b3475f9e7`) — every runner gates on these. **M1-C** masking = survivors'-mean fill (exact model-level fill-0); the masking confound is governed by the **PJ prefix-jitter control** (measured per-row jitter; TOST ±0.05), NOT by any numeric drift threshold — PJ is load-bearing for TRUCE masking. ## Repository conventions
- Canonical data: `data/processed/pairs.jsonl` (built by `dataset.py`). All downstream code reads this, never raw dataset layouts.
- SUSHI split: 8:1:1 stratified by `class_label`, seed 42, **frozen** — never regenerate.
- Per-series z-normalisation is a documented spec choice. **Note for Probe 3:** it removes mean and std at input, so summary-statistics sufficiency on CLaSP tests *shape-level* statistics.
- Batching is per-dataset (TRUCE 64, SUSHI 8) — mixed-length batches cause OOM and ~170× padding waste.
- Every experiment is a config-driven script writing JSON to `results/`. **No notebooks for runs** (exploration in notebooks is fine).
- Checkpoints stay out of Git (`results/checkpoints/` is ignored); result JSONs are committed.
- Scripts should **print their own diagnostics and state assumptions as gates that can fail**. Most errors in this project were caught by a script reporting something inconsistent about itself, not by a crash.
- Never add "Co-Authored-By" or "Authored by" trailers to commit messages.

## Facts that are easy to get wrong (pre-corrected — do not "fix" back)
- Tan et al. has **three** shuffle strategies (sf-all, sf-half, ex-half) plus a **separate** masking perturbation — not four shuffles.
- CLaSP has **no official code release**; `models/clasp/` is our reimplementation from the paper spec.
- CLaSP's published 0.458 / 0.982 are **soft** mAP@10 from **one joint model**; Table III rows are query sources, and its combined row is *not* a query-weighted mean (excluded from comparison).
- BEDTime authors are **Sen et al.** Per the current paper version (v3, 2026-04) it has **five** datasets incl. **TaxoSynth** and **NICU-HR** — say "one of its datasets", not "the fourth". *(Corrected 2026-08-18 with sign-off; the earlier "NICU-HR is fabricated" note applied to an earlier paper version — see docs/w1_verification_record.md. Cite v3.)*
- SUSHI's public release is the **Tiny** version (1,400 signals); Base is not publicly downloadable.
- Fons et al. Table 1 has **seven** univariate categories (stationarity is first-class).
- SUSHI labels are `<fluctuation>; <shape>` — 7 × 20 = 140 classes, a complete product.
- SUSHI clause rule: for non-`clean` classes the **last** sentence is the fluctuation clause and everything before it is the shape clause; for `clean` all sentences are shape. (Anchoring on the *first* sentence fails — cubic shapes take two sentences.)
