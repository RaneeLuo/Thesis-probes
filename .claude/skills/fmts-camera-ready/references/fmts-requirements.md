## Workshop

- **Name:** Foundation Models for Temporal Systems: From Forecasting to World Modeling (FMTS), NeurIPS 2026 workshop.
- **Date / place:** 2026-12-11, Sydney (room TBA). One-day, in person.
- **Research axes:** (1) forecasting and simulation tasks, (2) temporal data and environments, (3) temporal models,
  (4) evaluation and reliability. This project's paper (diagnostic evaluation of time-series–text alignment models)
  sits in axis 4: leakage-aware evaluation, robustness, reproducibility.
- **Reviewing criteria** (useful when answering reviews): technical quality; evaluation rigor (baselines,
  contamination/leakage awareness, statistical treatment); reproducibility (transparency, artifacts, documentation);
  relevance and impact.

## Format

- **Template:** official NeurIPS 2026 LaTeX template. NeurIPS publishes no separate workshop style file.
  Download: https://media.neurips.cc/Conferences/NeurIPS2026/Formatting_Instructions_For_NeurIPS_2026.zip
  (blocked from the cloud container; the `.sty` should already be in the user's submission source).
- **Submission page limit:** up to 4 pages, excluding references and appendices. Reviewers are not obliged to read
  appendices.
- **Camera-ready page limit:** `[UNVERIFIED]` The CfP states no camera-ready allowance. NeurIPS main-track camera-ready
  gets one extra page, but that does not automatically apply to workshops. Ask the user to check the OpenReview
  camera-ready form or acceptance email; until confirmed, target **4 pages** of main text.
- **Non-archival:** no proceedings. Accepted papers are posted on OpenReview and listed on the workshop site.
  Later publication at an archival venue is allowed.

## LLM / AI-assistant policy (NeurIPS 2026 main-track handbook, adopted by FMTS)

- LLMs and agents cannot be authors.
- Important, original, or non-standard use of an LLM or agent must be documented.
- Routine spelling, grammar, editing and basic code assistance need no disclosure.
- Authors remain responsible for all text, figures, claims and references.
- Prompt injection or attempts to manipulate reviewing are prohibited.
